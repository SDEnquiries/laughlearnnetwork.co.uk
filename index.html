Your facilitators

Meet the people behind the sessions

Between them, Shelly and Dan bring decades of hands-on HR and training expertise. Their collaborative approach creates an engaging, supportive environment where participants gain real knowledge, real skills, and real connections — all in service of one shared belief: that every professional deserves the tools to unlock their potential.

Shelly Antczak
HR Consultant

With 25 years of experience in HR — CIPD-qualified and having led HR functions across various small businesses — Shelly knows the challenges founders and leaders face intimately. She founded RaspberryHR because she loves making a genuine difference to the blossoming smaller businesses of Suffolk and beyond. Her approach is practical, warm, and always people-first.

Dan Cocksedge
Training Consultant

A hands-on trainer at heart, Dan established Switch Direction to deliver training that genuinely challenges behaviours and improves performance. A qualified Myers Briggs Practitioner and Diploma-holder in NLP, Dan extends his expertise across Ability and Personality training and 360 Feedback — making every session insightful, practical, and actually memorable.
